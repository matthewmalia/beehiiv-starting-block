# Voice and format (starter)

**Status: the writer's voice has not been captured yet.** This starter is uploaded by `/start` so a new writer can publish straight away. `/capture-voice` replaces it with a description of how this writer actually writes.

Until then, one rule matters more than any other: **the draft is the style guide.** The writer's wording, rhythm and habits are right by default. Don't improve them toward a general idea of good writing.

## Voice

- Keep the writer's sentences. Never rewrite a paragraph in a different voice.
- Treat anything the writer does more than once as deliberate: sentence fragments, very short paragraphs, lowercase headings, a recurring phrase, starting sentences with "And" or "But".
- Suggestions (never automatic changes) are welcome where a reader would be lost: an unclear sentence, a missing step, a point that takes too long to arrive. Keep them few.

## House style

Mechanical fixes are limited to:

- Plain errors: typos, misspellings, grammar slips, doubled words, broken punctuation.
- **Consistency with the draft itself.** Where the writer uses two forms of the same thing (email and e-mail, 10 and ten for the same kind of number, US and UK spellings), change the minority to match the majority and list it. If it's an even split, ask.
- Broken or untidy links, when the right destination is obvious.

Don't impose a rule the draft doesn't already follow. No changes to dashes, serial commas, capitalization of headings, or emoji use unless the draft is inconsistent with itself.

## Post layout

- No fixed opening or sign-off. Use the writer's own from each draft, exactly as written.
- Headings as the writer wrote them. Don't add headings to a post that has none.
- Images where the writer placed them, full width, with alt text.
- The post template holds only the body; nothing is added before or after the writer's text.

**Short format (for heavy weeks):** about 200 words, one idea, no images required.

## Packaging

- **Headline:** a plain statement of the post's idea, 60 characters or fewer. Use the writer's own title if the draft has one, and offer two alternatives.
- **Subject line:** about 50 characters or fewer, honest about what's inside. No all caps, no "RE:" tricks.
- **Preview text:** continues the subject line; never repeats it.
- **Images:** the writer's own only. **Generated thumbnails: not allowed** until the writer says otherwise.
- **Content tags:** one per post, reusing an existing tag where one fits. A new tag only with the writer's OK.
