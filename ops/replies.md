# Reader replies

Replies are the most useful signal you get, and the beehiiv MCP can't read your inbox. Paste notable replies here, or into the chat and the Publishing Lead adds them. The analyst includes them in the weekly report and the Lead turns them into suggested ideas.

**Leave out names and email addresses.** Paste what the reader said, not who they are.

## How to add one

```markdown
### 2026-12-02 · re: Why small lists win
> How do you find time to reply to everyone once the list grows?

Note (optional): third person to ask this.
```
