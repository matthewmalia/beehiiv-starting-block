# Content calendar

Planned with the writer in `/plan-content`. The Publishing Lead keeps it up to date, and `/weekly-review` checks it against beehiiv. If this file and beehiiv disagree about what was sent or when, beehiiv is right.

**Send:** <day> at <time> (<time zone>) · **Draft due:** <n> days before, <time> · **Cadence:** weekly

**Streak:** 0 on-time sends · **Sent:** 0

**Status:** `planned → editing → approved → scheduled → sent`, or `missed`.

A send is **on time** if it went out on the planned day, within an hour of the planned time. A short post sent on time keeps the streak.

## Slots

One row per send. Past rows stay: they are the publishing history.

| Send date | Send time | Draft due | Working title | Status | Actual send | On time | Folder | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

*Notes: `in inbox` for a post waiting in `inbox/`, `short post` when the late-draft short format went out, and anything else worth remembering.*
