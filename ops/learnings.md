# Learnings

Kept by the analyst. A finding starts as an `observation` (one post). When a second post supports it, it becomes `proposed`. When the writer approves it, the Publishing Lead adds it to the *Lessons learned* section of the `voice-and-format` skill and marks it `approved`. A later post that contradicts it makes it `retired`, with the reason in Notes.
