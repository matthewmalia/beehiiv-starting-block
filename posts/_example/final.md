# Why small lists win

Here's why small lists win.

When you have 200 readers, you can answer every email. Not with a canned reply. A real one. I keep [the welcome template I use](https://example.com/welcome-template) open in a tab, and every new subscriber gets a note from me within a day. Nobody with 200,000 readers can do that, and readers can tell.

A small list also shows its hand quickly. Ask a question and you get 12 answers by lunch, and nine of them say the same thing. That's your next post.

So here's the whole method. Start with the form, then share it everywhere. Watch the signups. Then email each new reader one question: what are you stuck on?

If you don't have a form yet, [start here](https://example.com/start). It takes 10 minutes, and your first email can go out today.

When I started replying to every new subscriber, my open rates doubled over my first eight weeks, from 31% to 62% (my own numbers). I didn't change my subject lines. I didn't change my send time. I just answered my email.

Two things I'd read next: [how I sort replies into post ideas](https://example.com/replies-to-ideas) and [the one-question survey I ask at signup](https://example.com/signup-survey). Both are short.

What are you stuck on with your list? Hit reply and tell me. I read everything.
